# TutorSlot Policies and FAQ

## Tutoring hours
Tutoring sessions are offered between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM, seven days a week. All times are
Pacific Time. Each tutor publishes their own open slots, so the exact times available change from
week to week. The Browse slots page always shows what is open right now.

## How booking works
Tutoring is free for enrolled students. Log in with your student account, open Browse slots, and
filter by tutor, subject, or date. Pick an open slot, add optional notes for your tutor (for
example, the topic you are stuck on), and confirm. You will see a confirmation page and receive a
confirmation email. Each slot can be booked by only one student. If two students try to book the
same slot at the same moment, only one booking succeeds and the other student is asked to choose
a different slot. Slots that have already started cannot be booked. You can also book through the
TutorSlot assistant, which always shows you the session details and asks you to confirm first.

## Cancellation policy
You can cancel a booked session at any time before the session starts, with no penalty. Cancel
from the My appointments page, or ask the assistant to cancel it for you. You will receive a
cancellation email, and the slot becomes open again for other students. A session that has
already started or finished cannot be cancelled.

## Late arrival and no-show policy
Your tutor waits up to 15 minutes after the start time. If you are more than 15 minutes late,
the tutor may end the session. Arriving late does not extend the session past its scheduled end
time. If you miss a session without cancelling, it is not rescheduled automatically; book a new
slot instead. Students with three or more no-shows in one semester may be asked to meet with the
Tutoring Center coordinator before booking again.

## Session length
Every tutoring session is 60 minutes long and is one on one: one tutor and one student. To get
more time on a topic, book two separate slots.

## Reminders
TutorSlot sends a reminder email within the 24 hours before each booked session. Each appointment
gets at most one reminder, and cancelled appointments get none. Reminders and confirmations go to
the email address on your student account.

## How to get help
The TutorSlot assistant can answer questions about these policies, find open slots, and book or
cancel sessions for you. For anything else, such as an account problem, email the Tutoring Center
at help@tutorslot.example or visit the Tutoring Center front desk during tutoring hours.
